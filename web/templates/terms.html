Terms of use

Short, and mostly about what the numbers mean.

What this tool produces

Every figure this site returns is the output of a kinematic simulation. It is not a measurement of any real machine, and it has not been validated against field trials. Several of its inputs, including steering response and side slope drift, are stated assumptions rather than published specifications. The catalog page lists all of them.

Do not rely on it alone

Results here must not be used as the sole basis for a machinery purchase, a guidance setting, an agronomic plan or any safety related decision. Validate against your own field measurements before acting.

Equipment names

Manufacturer and model names identify the machines whose published specifications the catalog cites. They are the trademarks of their respective owners. This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any manufacturer named in it.

Acceptable use

Please do not attempt to overload the service, work around the request limits, or use automated clients to run large sweeps against it. The command line tools in the source repository do that work far better, and running them locally costs you nothing.
